# OMIX vs. a Generic Web Agency: What Is the Difference?

**Category:** Why OMIX  
**Reading time:** 5 min

A conventional agency can be the right choice for a simple brochure site. The decision changes when software becomes part of how the business operates.

## Website delivery vs. system delivery

When a project requires data, roles, APIs, payments, automation or internal workflows, the deliverable is a system rather than a collection of pages.

## One architecture across the project

Frontend, backend, database and third-party services should have explicit boundaries. This reduces duplicated logic and makes future changes safer.

## Operations after launch

Deployment, domains, integrations, monitoring and maintenance belong to the product lifecycle. A production system should not become an orphan after the final design review.

## When an agency is enough

If you only need a simple marketing website, a specialist design agency may be the right fit. OMIX becomes more useful as the software itself becomes part of the business operation.
