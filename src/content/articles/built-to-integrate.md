# Built to Integrate: Why Integration Is a First-Class Requirement

**Category:** Why OMIX  
**Reading time:** 5 min

Modern businesses depend on connected services. Payments, messaging, identity, analytics, storage and business tools rarely live in one system.

## The modern stack is connected

Integration boundaries are therefore part of normal product architecture. Treating them as an afterthought usually creates fragile dependencies and duplicated logic.

## Design clear contracts

Stable APIs, validation, authentication and predictable error handling make integrations easier to test, monitor and replace.

## Reduce unnecessary vendor lock-in

A modular integration layer can isolate external providers. That makes it easier to change a service when pricing, capabilities or business requirements change.

## The OMIX principle

We design systems so the pieces can communicate without becoming inseparable. That is the practical meaning behind our integration-first approach.
